Before you start

  1. Every session opens with the 2-minute warm-up: 30s arm circles, 30s leg swings, 30s hip circles, 30s marching in place.
  2. Rest 45–60 seconds between sets. Finish each set with 1–2 reps still in the tank; clean form beats extra reps.
  3. Can't hit the numbers? Repeat the previous week's dose. Too easy? Slow the lowering phase to 3 seconds.
  4. Missed a day? Do the next one on your list. Don't stack two sessions to catch up.
  5. Sharp or joint pain means stop. Normal muscle soreness in week 1 fades by week 2.
