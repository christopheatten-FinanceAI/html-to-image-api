Before you start

  1. Every session opens with the 2-minute warm-up: 30s arm circles, 30s leg swings, 30s hip circles, 30s marching in place.
  2. Strength days use straight sets: do set 1 of the first exercise, rest 45–60s, set 2, rest, set 3, then rest once more and move to the next exercise. The timer walks you through it.
  3. The circuit day is different: one round is every exercise back to back on the timer, with only a short switch between them. Take a 1-minute break, then start the next round.
  4. Finish each set with 1–2 reps still in the tank. Clean form beats extra reps.
  5. Can't hit the numbers? Repeat the previous week's dose. Too easy? Slow the lowering phase to 3 seconds.
  6. Missed a day? Do the next one on your list. Don't stack two sessions to catch up.
  7. Sharp or joint pain means stop. Normal muscle soreness in week 1 fades by week 2.
